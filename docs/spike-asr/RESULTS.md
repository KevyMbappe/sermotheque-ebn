## What this sample proves
- **ASR is robust across the whole 2.7-year span and across speakers** — zero failures, theological French captured cleanly (even 1 Tim 3:16 verbatim).
- **Enrichment is "confirm-don't-type"** — topics & summaries are accurate and grounded; **series context is often recovered from the audio that the titles don't contain** (Confession de foi ch. 4 & ch. 8).
- **Errors are systematic** (🟢 pastor: `foi→fois`, `le→les`, `Dieu→dieux`) → a small regex post-clean fixes them; native-FR preachers (🔵) transcribe even cleaner.
- **Speaker is inferable as a *class*** (🟢 vs 🔵) from the artifact signature, but not by name — confirms a default-speaker rule is the right call.
